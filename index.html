// the core idea

Two things dashboards never show you.

Most compliance tools look for what's unusual. SAT-SA is built to look for what's missing — because in a SOC, what's missing is usually the thing that gets covered up first.

01 · EXECUTION GAP

Reported ≠ real

A control is documented as working, but the underlying case records say otherwise — a "policy" that exists on paper only.

02 · NEGATIVE SPACE

Evidence that should exist, and doesn't

A critical asset marked "monitored" that has produced zero telemetry for three weeks. Not an anomaly — an absence.

// detection engine

Eight explainable gap categories.

Every category is deterministic and versioned where possible — statistics and rules first, machine learning only as an optional pattern-discovery layer on top.

01

No escalation on a critical alert

Checked against a severity × asset-criticality escalation matrix.

02

Unusually fast closure

Compared to peer-group median and IQR, not a flat threshold.

03

Weak or boilerplate investigation

Scored on completeness, evidence count, and root-cause quality.

04

Repeat alerts, no remediation

Repeat ratio per asset — recurrence without a fix on record.

05

Templated case narratives

TF-IDF similarity across a CSE's investigation notes, offline.

06

Missing telemetry coverage

A "monitored" critical asset with near-zero observed events.

07

Peer-group deviation

Risk-adjusted using median/IQR — controls for size and criticality mix.

08

Cross-table inconsistency

Escalations, closures, and timelines checked for internal coherence.
