Beyond Surface Compliance: Why Conventional SOC Oversight Fails

NCIIPC evaluates the cyber resilience of Critical Sector Entities (CSEs) operating national infrastructure. Today, supervisory examiners face a critical structural gap between reported compliance and operational evidence.

The Compliance Illusion

  • Entities report 99.2% alert closure compliance and fast response times.
  • Self-assessment audits and static KPI dashboards show green across the board.
  • Examiners can manually inspect only 1–3% of submitted records via random spot-checks.
  • Random sampling consistently misses high-consequence failure modes.

The Operational Reality (The Two Core Breakthroughs)

1. Execution Gaps:

High closure compliance is achieved by closing critical alerts in <5 mins with zero evidence, no escalation, and boilerplate copy-paste notes.

2. Negative Space:

Uncovering what should exist but is missing: critical SCADA gateways producing zero telemetry or alerts for weeks.

Core Value Proposition: SAT-SA is an offline decision-support tool that transforms massive periodic SOC records into an explainable, evidence-backed supervisory review queue, prioritizing expert examiner attention without replacing human judgement.
